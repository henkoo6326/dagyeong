Bamboo scoop

Natural bamboo

A light, open scoop for moving loose tea from its packet to the pot. The natural curve of bamboo gives this small gesture a clear and simple form.

Material & form

Natural bamboo. Part of the DAGYEONG concept collection.

Care & use

Wipe gently and dry fully. Avoid soaking and prolonged direct heat.
