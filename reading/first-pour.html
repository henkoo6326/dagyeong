Before the first pour

A closer look at what fills a cup.

Look before you brew.

Tea offers small details before it meets water: the shape of a leaf, the scent inside a container, the sound it makes as it falls into a vessel. Paying attention can begin here, without needing to name everything you notice.

Follow curiosity.

You might be drawn to a fresh scent, a deeper roasted character, or the comfort of something familiar. Taste is a starting point for conversation, not a result to get right. Let your preferences change as you try different things.

Make room for difference.

The amount of leaf, the water and the time you give it can change the cup. Begin with the guidance for your chosen tea, then adjust gently towards what you enjoy. Keep the process simple enough to repeat.

Then, sit down.

The preparation is only the beginning. Give the cup a place in your day: beside a book, at the end of a meal, or between two people who have not seen each other for a while. The tea is there to accompany the time.
