A room of your own

The everyday art of being here.

A pause does not need a plan.

It might begin with moving a chair towards the window. Or setting down a cup before opening a book. The point is not to arrange the perfect moment, but to notice the one already available.

Let the room do less.

A clear surface, comfortable light and one useful object can be enough. When a room asks less of us, there is more space to hear what we need: quiet, company, or simply a few minutes without another task.

Familiar things make a place.

The cup you know by its weight. A table at the height you like. A view that changes a little every day. These small forms of familiarity can turn a corner of a room into somewhere that feels like your own.

Begin where you are.

There is no special equipment for taking your time. Start with the room you have and something you enjoy drinking. Leave the phone a little further away. Let the water settle. There is no need to make more of it.
