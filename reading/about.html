A place for the everyday.

We believe the way we spend our time matters — even when nothing remarkable is happening.

Our point of view

DAGYEONG begins with tea, but our interest reaches beyond the cup. We think about the objects we reach for, the rooms we settle into, and the people we choose to spend time with.

Our purpose is simple: to help everyday life feel a little more like your own.

Alone is enough.
Together is welcome.

Some days ask for quiet. Others call for an extra cup. Neither needs a ceremony, a perfect room, or a reason to begin.

We create around both. Useful objects with a generous presence. Tea that invites curiosity. Spaces that leave room for different ways of being.

Less performance.
More presence.

We are working towards a slower, more attentive relationship with the everyday. This is the question behind every object, story and space we imagine: does it make life more considered, without making it more complicated?
